ODIUM — standalone illustrated prologue

Scene: Scenes/BRATSceneStory.unity
Controller: Story / BRATGUIStoryIntro

Open the scene and press Play. The six chapters run automatically.
The scene contains its own camera, Canvas and EventSystem.
No gameplay managers or other BRAT modules are required.

Inspector:
- Chapters: title, English narration, illustration root, independent Layers and extra reading time.
- Layers: separate character/prop images and their small independent Travel offsets.
- Characters Per Second: typing speed (28 by default).
- Reading Hold: pause after the complete paragraph (3.5 seconds).
- Fade Duration: time to fade to/from black (1.25 seconds).
- Black Hold: pause between illustrations (0.3 seconds).
- On Completed: optional external action after the last chapter; intentionally unassigned.

Public actions:
- Play(): restart the whole prologue in Play Mode.
- Next(): advance to the next chapter through black, even during typing.
- SkipStory(): fade to black and invoke On Completed once. Does not load another scene.
- Stop(): cancel playback and restore the first chapter as a stable preview.

Next is at bottom right, Skip Story at bottom left, using the book's ink-stroke button style.
Replay appears after completion or Skip Story and calls Play().
All timing uses unscaled time. Disabling the controller cancels playback and resets the view.
Portrait layouts stack illustration above narration; landscape layouts place them side by side.

StoryNarration.txt is a readable copy of the initial story. Runtime text is serialized in Chapters.
Images/Layers/ contains eleven newly generated terrain-free dark painted subjects.
The six original combined illustrations remain in Images/ as unused references.
Textures/ contains technical lower-screen black gradients; the upper backdrop is unchanged.
Deep shadow dims the backdrop at 42% opacity. Individual layer Travel is five times the first layered draft.
Materials/BRATMaterialStoryColor adjusts illustration saturation and colour balance; layer Image tint is neutral white.
The colour material is used only by the eleven illustrations, not by the backdrop or text. Source PNGs remain unchanged.
LayerImagePrompts.json records the revised built-in image_gen prompts.
ImagePrompts.json records the first draft prompts and the original book references.
The old book is a visual reference only. Existing scene objects were not copied.
