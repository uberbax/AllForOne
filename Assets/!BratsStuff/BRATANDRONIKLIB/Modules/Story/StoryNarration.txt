1. The grave
As children, the archer and her brother had a knock of their own. Two taps, a pause, then one more. Three evenings after she buried him, she heard it under his headstone. She was on her knees, clawing at the earth, before she remembered the coins she had laid on his eyes.

2. What answered
The hunter lifted the grate. A horned demon crouched on the coffin, tapping the lid. His knife skidded off its hide. Then it laughed, and the laugh was her brother's. Hatred ran hot down the archer's bow arm. She knew that heat. Odium. Her arrow went clean through its hand.

3. The last watch
From his tower the watchman saw doors open all along the lane. A winged demon was leading the baker toward the old quarry by the hand. Three children followed with their coats on over their nightshirts. The watchman sounded the bell. Only those who had yet to hear a knock came running.

4. The oath
At the shrine the watchman named the missing. The old knight laid his sword on the altar. "I worked that quarry in chains. So did some of the demons you'll find in the crypt at Red Hook. They know a way in." He put his hand on the blade. "Those children will sleep in their own beds again."

5. The road in
The ranger's hound found flour on the bridge rail. Below it, a small bare footprint shone in the river mud. The captives were alive. The ranger stayed to follow their trail. The others took the road to Red Hook. This time, when her brother knocked from the trees, the archer kept walking.

6. Before the dawn
At the gates of Red Hook Estate the knight pinned the demon's wounded hand to the gatepost. The archer slipped inside and made straight for the crypt. "Which of you got out of the quarry?" she called down the steps. "I need someone willing to fight beside me. We're bringing those children home."
