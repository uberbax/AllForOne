1. The grave
In Odium, the dead were once given silence. Now even their graves whisper. Beside the last person she could not save, an archer swore that no more names would be surrendered to the dark.

2. What answered
The first hunters followed those whispers beneath the chapel. Something waited there, clothed in the shapes of their fears. Steel could wound it. Steel alone could not make it stay dead.

3. The last watch
When the watchfires failed, a few still held the heights. Their armour was broken; their names were forgotten. They kept the road open for those who had nowhere left to flee.

4. The oath
In a ruined shrine, a knight laid his sword before a dying flame. He asked for no crown, no forgiveness. Only the strength to stand when the next door opened.

5. The road in
A ranger and his hound found the trail the living had abandoned. Beyond the black pines stood the old keep. Beneath it lay the source of the voices. One by one, the survivors took the same road.

6. Before the dawn
At the gate, the thing from the graves rose to meet them. This time, no one faced it alone. They had come to reclaim their dead, and to learn what the darkness would demand in return.
